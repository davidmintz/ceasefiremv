# Charter of Martha's Vineyard for Palestine

*Adopted [DATE] by vote of the membership.*

## 1. Name

The name of this organization is **Martha's Vineyard for Palestine** ("the organization"), an unincorporated association.

## 2. Purpose

The organization is a political and educational group that supports the cause of Palestinian freedom and is explicitly anti-Zionist. It organizes protests and presents educational and cultural events, including film screenings, and works to educate its members and the broader public about the history and current events of Palestine and Israel.

## 3. Membership

There are no membership dues. Any person who attends a meeting of the organization and agrees with its core principles, as set out in Section 2 (Purpose), is deemed a member from that point forward. Membership carries no further obligation.

## 4. Meetings

**4.1 General meetings.** The organization holds meetings from time to time as called by the Steering Committee or by petition of any five members. Decisions of the membership are made by simple majority vote of members present.

**4.2 Annual meeting.** The organization holds an Annual Meeting each year for the purpose of electing the Steering Committee. The Annual Meeting shall be announced to the membership by email at least one month in advance.

**4.3 Remote attendance.** Attendance and voting by Zoom or other comparable remote video platform is permitted at any meeting, including the Annual Meeting, and counts the same as in-person attendance for purposes of membership, quorum, and voting.

## 5. Steering Committee

**5.1 Composition.** The organization is governed by a Steering Committee (also referred to as the Executive Committee) of five (5) members, elected by the membership.

**5.2 Election and term.** Steering Committee members are elected by simple majority vote of members present at the Annual Meeting, and serve a term of one year, renewable without limit. A vacancy on the Committee may be filled by a vote of the remaining Committee members until the next Annual Meeting.

**5.3 Authority.** The Steering Committee is empowered to make decisions on behalf of the organization, including but not limited to financial, operational, and administrative matters. It is the understanding and practice of the organization that the Committee will discuss significant decisions with the full membership where practical before acting, but the Committee's authority to act does not depend on obtaining prior membership approval.

**5.4 Officers and financial authority.** The Steering Committee shall designate from among its members a Treasurer and one additional Committee member as authorized signers on the organization's bank and financial accounts. Each authorized signer may independently open, maintain, and close accounts, sign checks, authorize transactions, and otherwise act on the organization's behalf in financial matters.

## 6. Amendments

This charter may be amended by a simple majority vote of members present at any meeting, provided the proposed amendment was announced to the membership in advance.

## 7. Dissolution

Should the organization dissolve, any remaining funds shall be distributed to one or more organizations with a similar charitable or educational purpose, as determined by a vote of the membership.
